Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Shoptie ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Shoptie.co.uk, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. The who

Shoptie is a fashion technology service operating from the United Kingdom. For any privacy questions you can email hello@shoptie.co.uk.

2. Images you upload

When you upload a photo of an outfit to the AI Stylist, the image is processed in your browser. In the current version of the service:

  • Your image is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Your image is not stored, displayed or shared by Shoptie.
  • The image is used only to generate colour- and style-based tie recommendations on your device.

If, in future, we connect a professional AI vision service, we will update this policy and seek your consent before any image is transmitted.

3. Information you send us

If you contact us via the contact form or email, we use your name, email address and message solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

4. Cookies

Shoptie.co.uk uses only the minimum technical storage needed for the site to function — for example, holding a styling session in your browser during your visit.

  • Session storage: temporarily holds your analysis results in your browser so the results page can display them. It is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Local storage: used for small preferences, such as remembering your chosen analysis provider between visits.

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will ask for your consent first.

5. How long we keep data

Session data is discarded when your session ends. Contact messages are kept for as long as needed to resolve your enquiry, and deleted afterwards.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact hello@shoptie.co.uk.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be announced on this page.
