How It Works

From outfit to perfectly matched tie

Shoptie turns an everyday decision into a confident one — in three simple steps.

01

Upload your outfit

Take a photo of your shirt, suit, coat — or a full outfit — and upload it to the AI Stylist. JPG, PNG and WEBP are all supported. Photos never leave your device.

02

AI reads your outfit

Shoptie analyses the dominant and secondary colours, identifies the pattern, judges the formality and estimates the clothing type. The result is a styling profile built around your actual outfit — not generic advice.

03

Discover and shop

You'll receive six tie recommendations, each with the colour, pattern, why it matches, styling advice and a recommendation score. Soon, each pick will link straight to UK retailers.

Behind the Scenes

How the analysis works today

Smart, private and instant

Shoptie's demo engine runs entirely in your browser. It extracts the true colour palette from your photo, compares it against a curated collection of tie styles, and scores each one on colour harmony, formality fit and contrast. No image is uploaded, stored or shared.

Built for real AI later

The analysis layer is designed so a professional AI vision service (for even sharper pattern and garment recognition) can be plugged in seamlessly — without changing the experience you see today.
