About Shoptie

The end of tie indecision

Shoptie is a British fashion technology brand building the smartest way to match a tie to an outfit.

Our story

It starts the same way for everyone: a wedding invitation, a job interview, a formal evening — and a wardrobe of perfectly good ties that suddenly feel impossible to choose between. We believed choosing a tie should feel like a decision already made.

So we built Shoptie: a styling engine that looks at your actual shirt, suit or coat and tells you — with confidence — which tie colours and styles will work, and why.

Why we focus on ties

Other tools guess from words like "navy" or "formal". Shoptie analyses your real outfit image — extracting the genuine palette and formality — and matches it against a curated collection of the ties that look best in UK business, weddings and events.

Our values

Honesty. Our demo engine is transparent about what it can and can't detect. Privacy. Images are processed locally and never stored. Craft. Recommendations are styled with the same care a Savile Row consultant would give.

Looking ahead

Shoptie is being built to partner with leading UK tie retailers and brands — turning every styling session into a lead that connects your personal style to the perfect product. If that sounds like your brand, we'd love to hear from you.
